📱 Permissions We Use

BachaLo requests certain permissions to provide emergency assistance and safety features.

📍 Location Permissions

  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION
  • ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION
  • ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION
  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION

Used to:

  • Obtain your current location during emergencies.
  • Share location information with trusted contacts.
  • Provide background location updates when emergency mode is active.

📩 SMS Permissions

  • SEND_SMS
  • RECEIVE_SMS
  • READ_SMS

Used to:

  • Send emergency alerts to trusted contacts.
  • Receive emergency-related SMS commands.
  • Support SOS communication features.

👥 Contacts Permission

  • READ_CONTACTS

Used to allow users to select trusted emergency contacts directly from their device.

📞 Phone & Call Information

  • READ_PHONE_STATE
  • READ_CALL_LOG

Used only for emergency-related features when enabled by the user.

🎤 Microphone Permission

  • RECORD_AUDIO

Used to detect emergency voice trigger commands such as:

  • "Help"
  • "Save Me"
  • "Bachao"

🔔 Notification Permission

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS

Used to display emergency alerts, service status updates, and safety notifications.

⚙️ Background Service Permissions

  • FOREGROUND_SERVICE
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED
  • WAKE_LOCK
  • REQUEST_IGNORE_BATTERY_OPTIMIZATIONS

Used to keep emergency protection services running reliably in the background.

🌐 Internet Permission

  • INTERNET

Used to support online functionality and service-related features when required.

🔒 Data Usage

BachaLo uses the above permissions only to provide emergency assistance and safety-related features.

We Do Not:

  • Sell personal information.
  • Rent personal information.
  • Share personal information with advertisers.
  • Create marketing profiles.
  • Upload personal information to our own servers for advertising purposes.

Most emergency-related information is processed locally on the user's device.

Data Collection

We do not sell or store personal data. Most processing occurs locally on the device.

📋 Call Log Permission

  • READ_CALL_LOG

Used only for emergency-related functionality when enabled by the user. Call log information is never sold, shared with advertisers, or used for marketing purposes.

♿ Accessibility Service

BachaLo may use Android Accessibility Service features to support emergency assistance functions requested by the user.

Accessibility access is used solely for safety-related actions, emergency automation features, and user-requested emergency functionality.

Accessibility data is not collected, sold, stored on external servers, or used for advertising purposes.

Accessibility Service is used exclusively for safety and emergency-related functionality requested by the user. It is not used to collect personal information, monitor user activity for advertising, or share data with third parties.

🛡️ Device Administrator Access

BachaLo may request Device Administrator privileges only for emergency protection features enabled by the user.

Device Administrator access is used solely to enhance safety features and prevent unauthorized interruption of emergency protection services.

Users can disable Device Administrator access at any time through Android device settings.

About BachaLo

BachaLo is a personal safety and emergency assistance application designed to help users communicate emergency information with trusted contacts.

BachaLo is not a replacement for police, ambulance, medical, fire, or other emergency services.

SMS Commands

Trusted contacts may use the following commands:

  • Hi, Hello – Requests the user's current location and emergency information.
  • Stop – Stops automatic location updates previously initiated through the App.

Only trusted contacts configured by the user can use these commands.
