1. About BachaLo

BachaLo is a personal safety and emergency assistance application designed to help users share emergency information, including location details and selected device information, with trusted contacts during emergency situations.

BachaLo is intended as a support tool only and is not a replacement for police, ambulance, fire, medical, or other emergency services.

2. Permissions Used by the App

  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS) – Used to select trusted emergency contacts.
  • SMS Permissions (SEND_SMS, RECEIVE_SMS, READ_SMS) – Used to send emergency alerts, receive emergency commands, and support SOS communication features.
  • Location Permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) – Used to obtain and share location information during emergencies.
  • Phone Information (READ_PHONE_STATE) – Used for emergency-related functionality when enabled by the user.
  • Call Log Access (READ_CALL_LOG) – Used only for emergency-related features and safety assistance functionality.
  • Notification Permission (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) – Used to display emergency alerts, safety notifications, and service status updates.
  • Microphone Access (RECORD_AUDIO) – Used for voice-trigger emergency features such as detecting phrases like "Help", "Save Me", or "Bachao".

    • The App may allow users to create custom emergency keywords for voice-triggered emergency activation. These keywords are configured by the user and processed locally on the device.

  • Accessibility Service – Used to support user-requested emergency actions and accessibility-based safety features. Accessibility access is never used for advertising or data collection purposes.
  • Device Administrator Access – May be used for user-enabled safety features that help prevent interruption of emergency protection services. Users can disable this access at any time through Android settings.
  • Battery Optimization Exemption – Helps emergency monitoring and protection services continue functioning reliably in the background.
  • Background Services (FOREGROUND_SERVICE, RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED, WAKE_LOCK) – Used to keep safety services active and restart emergency protection features after device reboot.
  • Internet Access (INTERNET) – Used to support app functionality, service reliability, and future online features when required.

3. SMS Commands

Trusted contacts may use specific SMS commands:

  • Hi, Hello – Requests the user's current location and emergency information.
  • Stop – Stops automatic location updates.

These commands are processed only when received from trusted contacts configured by the user.
